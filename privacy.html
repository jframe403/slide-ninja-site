Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Slide Ninja is a photo and video slideshow app made by Jason Frame. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Data not collected

Slide Ninja does not collect personal data. It does not create an account, and it does not include ads, analytics, or tracking. The app does not make network requests and does not send your content to us or to any third party.

Photos and videos

The app asks for access to your Photos library so it can play the albums you choose, full screen, on this iPhone. Photos and videos stay on your device. We cannot see them, and they are not uploaded, copied, or shared by the app.

If a photo or video is stored only in iCloud, Apple may download it to this iPhone so it can be shown. That transfer is between your device and Apple, not to us.

Settings stored on your iPhone

Preferences such as slide timing, transitions, and mute are saved in standard iPhone storage on this device so they are still there the next time you open the app. They are not sent anywhere.

Children

Slide Ninja is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect data from anyone.
