Slide Ninja

A full-screen photo and video slideshow for iPhone. Private and offline.

Slide Ninja turns a photo album on your iPhone into a full-screen slideshow. Photos fade or slide on a timer you choose. Videos play through with sound, then the show moves on. You can mute, pause, and keep the screen awake — all on this device.

The app works completely offline. It reads your Photos library on your iPhone and never sends your pictures or videos anywhere. There is no account, no ads, and no tracking.
