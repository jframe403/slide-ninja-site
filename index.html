Slidio

A full-screen photo and video slideshow for iPhone. Private and offline.

Slidio turns a photo album on your iPhone into a full-screen slideshow. Photos fade or slide on a timer you choose. Videos play through with sound, then the show moves on. You can mute, pause, and keep the screen awake — all on this device.

The app reads your Photos library on your iPhone and never sends your pictures or videos anywhere. There is no account, no ads, and no tracking. Playback works on this device. Subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple; the app does not collect payment data.
