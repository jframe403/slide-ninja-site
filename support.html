Support

Need help with Slide Ninja? Start with the notes below, or email jif403@gmail.com.

Allowing photo access

Slide Ninja needs access to your Photos library to play an album. When the app asks, choose Allow Full Access or Limited Access.

If you chose Limited Access, albums only include the photos and videos you selected. You can add more from the banner on the album list, or change access in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security > Photos > Slide Ninja.

If access is off, open that same Settings screen and turn it on.

Subscriptions

You can browse your albums for free. Starting a slideshow requires a Slide Ninja Premium subscription (monthly or yearly).

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple, not through Slide Ninja. The app does not collect payment data. To subscribe, use the Subscribe button on the paywall. To restore a purchase on a new iPhone, tap Restore Purchases on the paywall or in Settings.

To change or cancel a subscription, open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions, or use Manage Subscription in Slide Ninja Settings. Billing and refunds are handled by Apple.

Photos stored in iCloud

If an item is stored only in iCloud, Slide Ninja shows a spinner while it downloads to this iPhone, then plays it. The next few slides are prefetched so the show can keep moving.

Keep the screen awake

Keep Screen Awake is on by default so the phone does not sleep during a slideshow, including while you are paused. Tap the Awake pill on the player, or use the toggle in Settings, to turn it off.

Video sound and mute

Videos play all the way through with sound, then the slideshow moves on. Tap the speaker pill on the player to mute or unmute, or turn on Mute Videos in Settings. Your choice is saved on this iPhone.
